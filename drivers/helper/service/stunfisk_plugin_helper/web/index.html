Set it up

  1. Load the extension. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. In Firefox, open about:debugging, choose Load Temporary Add-on, then in about:addons allow site access for Showdown. Pick the extension folder from the repository.
  2. Open Showdown. The official site or one served from your own computer. A box titled Stunfisk Coach appears in the bottom right corner.
  3. Sign in. Enter your Showdown username on this page. Your browser, logged in to Showdown as that name, approves it by itself. Or use your email: the service sends a six-digit code. There is no password either way.
  4. Open a battle. The helper reads it from the page and shows a suggested play.

Where your battle goes

On this computer: nothing leaves it. The extension talks to this service on 127.0.0.1.

On a remote service: the battle you have open is sent to that service. Choose one you trust.

The helper only reads. It never submits a move to Showdown.
